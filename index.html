    FROSTDRAGON TYRANT

    🐉 FDT Guide

    Battle preparation, assignments and phase-by-phase reminders.

    1. Before battle

    • Sign-up (48h): the Alliance Leader or an R4 registers. Only a limited number of top alliances per Zone qualify.
    • Members must already be in the alliance before sign-up ends, or they cannot be dispatched.
    • Dispatch (48h): R4/R5 select up to 60 combatants per alliance. Players not dispatched can still help by healing troops.
    • Eve of Battle (59h): finalize the Tyrant candidate, Rally Captains, Spire teams, Dragonite collectors and communication calls.

    2. Battle timeline

    • Battlefield is open for 7 hours. Cities start in the Border Region.
    • The Capital becomes vulnerable 1 hour after opening, leaving a 6-hour Capital battle.
    • Frostdragon Escorts appear in the Border Region when the Capital opens and refresh every 30 minutes.
    • Dragonite gathering appears 15 minutes after the Capital opens and also refreshes every 30 minutes.
    • Spires alternate 30 minutes vulnerable and 30 minutes protected.
    • Rearmament follows the battle for 55 hours: +500% Healing Speed and -30% Healing Costs.

    3. Spires (30-minute alliance buff)

    Life Spire: +20% Troop Health. Shield Spire: +20% Troop Defense. Dragonbane Spire: +20% Troop Lethality. Blade Spire: +20% Troop Attack. The alliance that dominates a Spire during its vulnerable period gets the buff for 30 minutes.

    4. Becoming Frostdragon Tyrant

    The Chief with the longest total Capital occupation time wins; on a tie, whoever reached that time first. Only the Rally Captain accumulates occupation time, reinforcers do not, so pick one candidate and avoid unnecessary captain changes. Tyrant privileges last 30 days.

    5. Common mistakes

    • Rotating random Rally Captains, which splits occupation time.
    • Ignoring Spires or leaving Capital Defense uncovered.
    • Not assigning anyone to Dragonite gathering and its 30-minute refresh.
    • Staying in the Dragon Crypt expecting a Shield: Shields are removed there and cannot be activated.
    • Spending all healing resources before Rearmament.

    6. Personal points

    Personal Points come from Carnage Points (severely injuring enemy troops) and Casualty Points (your troops severely injured) in Capital and Spire battles. Sitting in a building without fighting does not earn points.

    7. Task duties

    Every Battle player has one group task (Alliance A or B) plus one or more role tasks. Check your task on the roster before the Eve of Battle and follow your group leader's calls.

    • Rally Captain: starts and leads the Capital rallies and is the only one who accumulates occupation time. Stay on the Capital, keep the garrison full, and do not hand over the captain role unless the leader calls it.
    • Tyrant Candidate: the Chief the alliance is backing to become Frostdragon Tyrant. Hold the Capital as Rally Captain for as long as possible, avoid unnecessary captain changes, and keep your troops healed.
    • Capital Reinforcer: send your troops into the Rally Captain's Capital garrison and keep them there. Refill the garrison right after losses and do not leave the Capital while the captain is holding it.
    • Capital Defense: protect the Capital against enemy rallies and attacks. Stay close to the Capital, join defensive reinforcements when the captain is under pressure, and make sure the Capital is never left uncovered.
    • Spire Life: take and hold the Life Spire (+20% Troop Health) during its 30-minute vulnerable window. Coordinate with your group so the buff is secured as soon as the window opens.
    • Spire Shield: take and hold the Shield Spire (+20% Troop Defense) during its vulnerable window, and keep enough troops there to stop the enemy from retaking it.
    • Spire Dragonbane: take and hold the Dragonbane Spire (+20% Troop Lethality) during its vulnerable window. Move on to the next objective once the window closes.
    • Spire Blade: take and hold the Blade Spire (+20% Troop Attack) during its vulnerable window, and rally or reinforce as needed to keep it.
    • Dragonite Collector: gather Dragonite as soon as it appears (15 minutes after the Capital opens) and check again at every 30-minute refresh. Escort or hide gatherers if enemies are nearby. Collectors are the lowest-power players and multitask: until Dragonite opens (75 minutes after the Battlefield opens) they carry out their other duty, usually Capital Reinforcer.
    • Alliance A / Alliance B: your battle group. Players join the Capital, Spires and Dragonite calls of their own group. A player belongs to only one group.
    Tip: verify event-specific mechanics in-game before battle.
